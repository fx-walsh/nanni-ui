Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 12, 2026

At Nanni, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard the information you provide when utilizing our childcare coordination and notification assistant platform.

1. Information We Collect

To provide automated childcare logging services (such as task reminders and routine schedules), we collect the following minimum operational data:

  • Mobile Phone Number: Collected solely to deliver user-requested RCS/SMS task alerts and logging confirmation messages.
  • Message Transcripts: Custom conversational text updates sent by the user (e.g., feeding details, to-do completions) to execute helper tracking logic.
  • System Metadata: Timestamps associated with messages to handle exact notification delivery routines.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is explicitly used to operate, maintain, and provide the interactive notification utilities of Nanni. We utilize these data tracks to parse scheduling intents and return immediate messaging confirmations regarding your care workflows.

3. Mobile Information Protection Mandate

Mobile Information Sharing: No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with or sold to any third parties.

4. Data Retention

We maintain information only for as long as necessary to support your childcare log history and execution profiles. You may request the absolute deletion of your configuration space at any time by contacting support or opting out of the active loop.

5. Your Choices & Opt-Out

You control your interactions with Nanni at all times. To permanently cancel your alert cycles, you may reply STOP to any mobile message. For additional context or assistance, reply HELP.
